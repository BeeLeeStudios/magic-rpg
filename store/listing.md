# Google Play store listing – Dragons vs Math

Copy/paste into **Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.
Character limits are Google's; counts are included so edits stay inside them.

## App name (max 30)

```
Dragons vs Math
```
(15 characters. Optional longer form, 29 chars: `Dragons vs Math: Kids Math RPG`.
Google allows a short descriptor but rejects keyword stuffing, emoji, ALL CAPS and
claims like "#1" or "Best" in the title.)

## Short description (max 80)

```
Solve math to power up your dragon! Adaptive + − × ÷ practice for ages 5–13.
```
(76 characters)

## Full description (max 4000)

```
Every right answer is a hit. Every monster needs a mathematician.

Dragons vs Math is a pixel-art battle adventure where kids practice addition, subtraction, times tables and division by fighting goblins, slimes, skeletons and giant bosses. Pick a hero, earn gold, and ride into battle on a dragon — powered entirely by math.

MATH THAT FITS YOUR CHILD
• Starts tiny (numbers 1 to 4) and climbs one small step at a time, through two-digit carrying and borrowing, then times tables and division.
• Adapts automatically: the game moves up only after steady, accurate, fluent answers, and quietly eases off if your child is struggling — no "you failed" screens.
• Wrong answers teach: the correct answer lights up so every mistake becomes practice.
• Mastered skills come back for review so they aren't forgotten.
• Speed bonuses reward real fluency, not guessing.

AN ADVENTURE KIDS WANT TO PLAY
• 100 pixel monsters and 15 bosses with their own tricks — one splits into minions, one steals your gold, one asks riddles, one throws your mistakes back at you, and more.
• Cross five worlds on the world map: volcano, lagoon, forest, haunted night and frozen north.
• Watch your dragon grow from a little hatchling into a golden Elder.
• Fill the Dragon Book with every monster and treasure, and earn 20 badges.
• Save up gold for 10 dragon mounts with their own breath attacks, pets that fight alongside you, and five swords.
• Fireballs, potions, level-up rewards and treasure chests.
• Up to 12 save slots so brothers, sisters and classmates each keep their own progress and math level.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts.
• Collects no data at all, and works completely offline — the app doesn't even use the internet.
• A parent area (behind a grown-up question) shows each child's skills, accuracy and time played.
• Read-aloud problems, bigger text, reduce-motion and colour-blind options.
• Designed for ages 5–13.

Solve it. Beat it. Level up!
```

## Graphics (all generated by `node scripts/generate-store-assets.mjs`)

| Play Console field | File | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| App icon | `store/icon-512.png` | 512×512, 32-bit PNG |
| Feature graphic | `store/feature-graphic.png` | 1024×500, 24-bit PNG |
| Phone screenshots (2–8) | `store/screenshots/phone-*.png` | 1080×1920 (9:16) |

Tablet screenshots are optional; if you add them, use the same script with a
tablet viewport (7" = 1200×1920, 10" = 1600×2560).

## Other listing fields

* **App category:** Game → Educational (Play also lets you add tags such as "Math", "Kids").
* **Contact email:** required, shown publicly — use a support address, not a personal one.
* **Website:** optional.
* **Privacy policy URL:** the public URL where you host `docs/privacy-policy.html`.
